Summary

PlanDrop helps you identify places from screen content you choose to capture. Captured screen frames and text clues are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded to a PlanDrop service.

Screen capture and on-device analysis

PlanDrop asks you to choose Share Entire Screen through Apple’s system screen-sharing picker. When capture is active, iOS displays its system recording indicator.

PlanDrop temporarily retains recent screen frames and text clues on your iPhone while a capture is active. Apple’s on-device system language model performs place analysis. PlanDrop does not send captured frames or extracted OCR text to a PlanDrop cloud service.

You can use Stop & Delete Capture in PlanDrop or its Live Activity at any time. Temporary evidence is also cleared after you save a place.

Saved places

If you save a Drop, PlanDrop stores the saved place name, address or locality when available, category, map coordinates when available, verification status and saved date locally on your iPhone. You can delete individual saved Drops in the app.

PlanDrop does not provide an account, cloud backup, advertising, analytics or tracking service.

Maps and external search

PlanDrop may send a detected place name and locality to Apple Maps to check a possible match. It does not send captured screen frames or OCR text to Apple Maps.

If you choose Search Google, PlanDrop opens Google with a place query. Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply after you leave PlanDrop.

Changes and contact

Updates will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or requests, email ritcomapps@gmail.com.

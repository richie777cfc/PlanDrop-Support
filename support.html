Support

How can we help?

For assistance, email ritcomapps@gmail.com. Include the copied device support details from PlanDrop, but never send captured Reel frames or private screen content.

Capture does not start

Confirm that your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, that Apple’s on-device model is ready, and that you selected Share Entire Screen in the system picker.

PlanDrop cannot identify a place

Replay the part of the content that shows the venue name, storefront, menu branding or location, then use Drop this Reel. PlanDrop intentionally returns no result when evidence is insufficient or conflicting.

Stop and remove capture evidence

Use Stop & Delete Capture from PlanDrop’s More menu or Live Activity. Saved place details can be removed separately from Saved Drops.
